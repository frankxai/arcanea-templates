# Marketplace Release Contract

Each child template owns product.manifest.yaml and marketplace.listing.yaml. This catalog records only verified child state and links; it does not invent deployment, pricing, support or acceptance.

Before listing, prove clean setup, sample outcome, design quality, accessibility, performance, SEO, forms/errors, asset rights, license, compatibility, support, versioning and a current preview. Capture the platform listing ID, URL, submitted version, public price/license and verification time after publication.

Automations may audit drift and draft listing packets. Public submission, price changes, paid promotion and partner claims require explicit authorization.
